# Membrane Simulation Workflow

This page outlines the major stages of a typical membrane simulation project.

## Workflow stages

1. **Define the scientific question**  
   Identify the membrane composition, conditions, and biological or physical process of interest.
2. **Choose the simulation model**  
   Decide whether the system is better suited to a coarse-grained or atomistic description.
3. **Build the membrane system**  
   Construct symmetric or asymmetric bilayers with physiologically relevant compositions.
4. **Run the simulation**  
   Prepare inputs, equilibrate the system, and perform production simulations.
5. **Analyze the output**  
   Connect simulation observables back to the original question.
