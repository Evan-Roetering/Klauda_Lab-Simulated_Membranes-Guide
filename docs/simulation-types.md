# Simulation Types

This page provides a high-level guide to the major simulation choices referenced throughout this repository.

## Coarse-grained simulations

Coarse-grained models are useful when you need to study larger systems, longer time scales, or broader membrane behavior at reduced resolution.

## Atomistic simulations

Atomistic models are useful when detailed molecular interactions, local structure, or fine-grained interpretation are important.

## Symmetric bilayers

Symmetric bilayers are often a simpler starting point for learning setup, equilibration, and interpretation.

## Asymmetric bilayers

Asymmetric bilayers are useful when biological realism requires different leaflet compositions.
